The Ministry of Culture is committed to making its internet, intranet and extranet websites, software packages, mobile applications and digital urban furniture accessible in accordance with Article 47 of Law No. 2005-102 of 11 February 2005.

To this end, the Ministry is implementing the following strategy and actions:

- [Government Multi-Year Accessibility Plan](https://www.info.gouv.fr/accessibilite)
- [Ministry of Culture Multi-Year Digital Accessibility Plan](https://www.culture.gouv.fr/accessibilite-partiellement-conforme/schema-pluriannuel-d-accessibilite-numerique)

This accessibility statement applies to the **Euphrosyne** website (<https://euphrosyne.beta.gouv.fr>).

## Compliance status

**Euphrosyne** is not compliant with version 4 of the French General Accessibility Improvement Framework (RGAA). Accessibility recommendations have been taken into account throughout development; the official score will be communicated following the audit scheduled for 2026.

## Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was drawn up on 01/03/2023. It was last updated on 11/09/2026.

## Feedback and contact

If you are unable to access any content or service, you can contact the person in charge of Euphrosyne to be directed to an accessible alternative or to obtain the content in another format.

- Contact the service manager, Claire Pacheco ([claire.pacheco@culture.gouv.fr](mailto:claire.pacheco@culture.gouv.fr)); otherwise, contact the Collections product line of the Ministry of Culture’s Digital Service ([collections.snum@culture.gouv.fr](mailto:collections.snum@culture.gouv.fr)).

## Remedies

If you notice an accessibility issue preventing you from accessing content or a feature on the site, report it to us. If you do not receive a satisfactory response, you have the right to submit your complaint or a request for intervention to the Défenseur des droits (Defender of Rights).

Several options are available to you:

- Send a message to the [Défenseur des droits](https://formulaire.defenseurdesdroits.fr/formulaire_saisine/).
- Contact the [Défenseur des droits delegate in your region](https://www.defenseurdesdroits.fr/carte-des-delegues).
- Send a letter by post (free of charge, no stamp required):
